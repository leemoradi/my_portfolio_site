About Me

I'm a junior at Tufts University majoring in Philosophy and Political Science. My academic interests center on political theory, legal thought, and the overlap of philosophical ideas with current events. I'm especially drawn to questions about justice, rights, and power, and how they shape the legal and political systems we live under.

Outside of academics, I am passionate about activism, particularly around healthcare accessibility and reproductive justice. I am interested in how policy and philosophy intersect in these areas, and how lived experience informs both. I have also developed an interest in coding and data analysis, allowing me to approach complex social and political questions with both analytical rigor and computational tools.
